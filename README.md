# 💰 Fundlish

### *Financial Freedom for Every Woman*

</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

### The Problem
- **78%** of women weren't taught about money growing up
- Women earn **82¢** to men's dollar and live **6-8 years longer** with less retirement savings
- Financial education is male-dominated and inaccessible
- Generic advice ignores cultural contexts and women's unique challenges

### My Solution
An AI-powered platform that meets women exactly where they are—providing personalized, culturally-aware financial education that actually works.

### ✨ Core Features

🤖 **FundlishAI** → 24/7 personal money mentor powered by AI 
🌍 **Global-First** → 150+ currencies, 40+ languages, cultural intelligence  
🎮 **Money Simulator** → Practice negotiations and investments in a safe space  
👭 **The Circle** → Community accountability groups  
📚 **Personalized Paths** → Life-stage specific content (immigrant journey, single parent, entrepreneur)  
💎 **Beautiful Design** → Magazine-quality UX because we deserve better

**Tech Stack:** React Native · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · AI/ML Personalization

**Timeline:** Starting MVP development → Expected launch Q2 2026

# 🌸 The Side Project Collection

### 🗺️ **Wanderlist**
*Trip planning for how women actually travel*

Safety ratings by real women travelers, Instagram-worthy spot finder, group trip coordination, budget tracking with currency conversion, and local women-owned business discovery.

**Tech:** React Native, Google Maps API, AI recommendations  
**Status:** Early prototype phase

---

### 💕 **ChemPhlly** 
*Science-based dating for Philadelphia*

Personality compatibility using attachment theory and love languages, Philly-specific date recommendations, safety features, and anti-ghosting accountability. Quality connections over endless swiping.

**Tech:** React Native, Custom ML Algorithm, PostgreSQL  
**Status:** Algorithm development, beta Q3 2026

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🌸 **Callyx**
*Period tracker that protects your data*

Post-Roe reality demands privacy. Callyx offers period & symptom tracking, fertility awareness, and cycle syncing tips with **zero data sharing**. Your data lives on your device, encrypted and yours alone.

**Tech:** React Native, Local Storage Only, E2E Encryption  
**Status:** beta Q3 2026, security audit in progress

---

### 💸 **Coinette**
*Customizable budget tracker with depth*

AI spending insights, visual goal tracking, bill reminders, investment integration, and a guilt-free "treat yourself" fund. The goldilocks solution: not too simple, not overwhelming.

**Tech:** React Native, Plaid Integration, Custom Visualizations  
**Status:** Feature planning phase

</td>
